## Operator recovery

An unavailable receipt caused by unconfirmed shutdown includes the run ID and root. Stop the original executor first, then use:

```sh
cognia-agent worker settle --run-id RUN_ID --executor-stopped
```

The explicit assertion is required. Recovery drops the uncertain capture as Failed; it does not invent a trustworthy diff or Undo operation. Parent and child roots remain fenced until recovery succeeds.
