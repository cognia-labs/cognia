## Problem frame

Phase 0a builds the measuring instruments for the convergence: a trustworthy gate baseline (A1), member inventories that make debt countable and ratcheted (A2, A3), a harness that runs one behaviour on both hosts (A4), and two matrices that tell later phases what to move (A5, A6). Success is instruments that catch every known miss named in the plan, numbers reconciled with the audit, and growth that cannot slip in unnoticed. Fixing red gates (B9) and moving runtimes (S1, phases 1–4) are out of frame.
