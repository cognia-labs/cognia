## Problem frame

<!-- One paragraph: the problem we are choosing to solve now, the outcome that
would show it is solved, and what is explicitly out of frame. Cite IDs. -->

Audit fresh startup, local profile encryption and remote-person binding, then distinguish account lock from independent sleep mechanisms (G1, O1). Produce a platform/build flow matrix, reproducible defects, design trade-offs and follow-up verification obligations. The later repair request G2 authorizes surgical implementation and isolated regression verification of confirmed findings. Fresh-client product redesign, changed idle security policy, account migration and real-account login/logout remain out of frame (C1, C2).
