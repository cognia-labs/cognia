## Problem frame

<!-- One paragraph: the problem we are choosing to solve now, the outcome that
would show it is solved, and what is explicitly out of frame. Cite IDs. -->

Make the catalog usable and maintainable (G1, G2): document its real contracts, add representative missing visual states, resolve startup/build blockers, and connect real render/play/accessibility/screenshot checks to CI. Use existing components, providers, stores, Jest and Playwright; retain the full normal catalog and native/provider boundaries (C1). Reproduced accessibility and code-fidelity defects are corrected at their owning production component/token rather than hidden in stories. Whole-catalog rendering and real native/provider business acceptance are outside the selected-scene evidence.
