Squads and team conversations behave as their settings say. Squad settings and the roster now name the capability ids that no longer resolve, such as a skill whose plugin was disabled, and the review before a run lists those ids. A Squad can use the general-purpose subagent without being flagged. Stopping one member of a team conversation now interrupts that member's reply. Stopping a conversation from a paired phone clears every member's status. A team conversation never runs more automatic rounds than the editor allows, including for teams saved with a larger number.
