The artifact dock now keeps each conversation's requests to that conversation. A file or review link that resolves after you switch conversations no longer fills the new conversation's dock, a background agent's browser request waits for its own conversation instead of driving the one on screen, and proposals in a background conversation mark that conversation instead of raising the dock in front of you. Side chat opens from the tablet-width artifact sheet, and a historical review stays selected after the dock has been closed for a while.
