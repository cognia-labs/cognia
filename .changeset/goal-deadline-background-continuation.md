A /goal or self-paced /loop keeps going when you switch to another conversation, and its result card lands in its own conversation. A goal's time limit now stops the running turn when it is reached instead of waiting for the turn to end, in the app, the CLI and headless runs. Built-in chats without the sidecar follow the configured step limit instead of a fixed 8 steps.
