A plan approved in chat now runs as the turn it starts: it shows as running, completes when that turn finishes, and pauses with Resume / Mark done if the turn fails or is stopped. Deleting a conversation also deletes its plans, refining a plan keeps its non-chat steps, a repeated plan-mode event no longer replaces an approved plan, and the workflow plan-refine node no longer offers the inert "Judge deviation" trigger.
