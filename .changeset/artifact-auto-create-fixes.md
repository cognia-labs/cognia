Artifacts detected in a reply are now created reliably. Missing artifact settings no longer break detection, a conversation that finishes in the background still gets its artifacts and targeted edits, new artifacts belong to that conversation's project, and switching accounts mid-turn no longer drops another account's artifacts into yours.
