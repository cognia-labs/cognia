The quick actions on the phone home screen are now a single tidy row, like an app grid: each shortcut's artwork sits on a soft rounded tile with a short label underneath, and more than four shortcuts wrap to a second row. With the ordinary icon style, the shortcuts are bare line icons with no grey tile behind them; the soft plate stays only behind the illustrated icons.
