The title bar and the window title now name the page you are on — "Settings", "Workflows" — instead of always showing the last conversation, and on other pages a running conversation shows in the count badge rather than as the current page's activity. On narrower windows, title-bar controls that do not fit (such as the workspace switcher) now move into a "…" menu instead of disappearing. The layout menu no longer repeats theme and language, which stay in the View menu and Settings; zoom stays there.
