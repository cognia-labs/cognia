Smoother conversation opening and switching. Switching conversations no longer flashes the home screen first, and the new transcript fades in before its first frame is painted while the header and composer stay still. A long conversation shows its latest messages right away and loads the rest of its history in the background. A short one mounts only the rows near the bottom and adds earlier rows as you scroll up, without losing your place. Tool and history entrance animations now play only for replies that are still streaming in.

On a paired browser or the phone's remote session view, earlier turns load automatically as you scroll up (the "Load older turns" button is gone), and the transcript opens at the latest turn and follows a live reply. Tool calls are easier to read: inside an open "Worked for …" fold, small tool groups show their rows directly. Open/closed choices are remembered while scrolling and after a reply finishes. The "collapsed" tool display preference now also closes tool groups, background shell commands show as backgrounded instead of running, and short commands are no longer printed twice.
