Typing in the composer does less work per keystroke: the `@` / `/` completion panel runs its searches only while it is open, and the save-as-template dialog is built only when opened. The composer and its toolbar also no longer re-render when the chat around them changes without affecting them, and on a phone the notices stacked above the input are capped by the space left above the keyboard.
