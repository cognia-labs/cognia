Opening a conversation for the first time now shows that it is loading: a thin bar sweeps across the top of the chat and the previous conversation dims until the new one appears. A message sent while a long conversation is still loading its full history now shows a status strip on the composer until it goes out. A split pane, or the conversation kept on screen during that wait, no longer jumps up from the bottom when focus moves to another conversation.
