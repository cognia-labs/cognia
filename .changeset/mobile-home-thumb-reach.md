The phone home screen is calmer and easier to reach. The message box now sits at the bottom, just above the tab bar, where it stays when the conversation starts, instead of a quarter of the way down the screen. The greeting and your home cards scroll above it, each card on its own. Usage, activity and recent conversations join the home screen once there is something to show, and new phones start with shortcuts the screen doesn't already offer (Inbox, Templates, Squads, Devices). The top bar's title on the home screen is now your workspace's name (long names are shortened; tap it to switch) instead of "Cognia" beside a bare folder icon. The bar keeps the menu, workspace, search and ⋮: Glance moves into ⋮ on phones, and Job Center shows only while it has work (otherwise it's in ⋮ too).
