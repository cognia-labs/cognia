Open Feishu and Google documents in the chat dock: one tab per document with a read-only content snapshot (refresh with an explicitly chosen account, sent and latest versions kept apart) and the live original page in a separate document browser that keeps its own sign-in, loads no extensions and is invisible to agents. Document links, Sources rows, the `@lark:`/`@gdoc:` picker's Preview button, staged and sent cloud-document attachments, and created Lark documents all open there; "Attach to chat" stages a previewed document through the usual checks.
